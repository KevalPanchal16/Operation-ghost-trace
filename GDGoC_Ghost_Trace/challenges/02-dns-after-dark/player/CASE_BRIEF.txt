OPERATION GHOST TRACE | CASE 02: DNS AFTER DARK
Classification: Training simulation | Data: Entirely synthetic

INCIDENT
A fictional lab sensor captured a short burst of DNS traffic after an
internal endpoint began making unusual lookups. The capture contains normal
background traffic and a small amount of synthetic suspicious activity.

YOUR OBJECTIVE
Analyze dns_trace.pcap and recover the flag sent through DNS query names.
Identify the relevant client and DNS zone, reconstruct the fragments in the
correct order, and use the evidence in the capture to reverse the encoding.

SUBMISSION
Submit the recovered flag exactly in the format GDG{...}.

SCOPE & SAFETY
This is an offline packet-analysis exercise. All packets are synthetic.
The capture uses documentation-only IPv4 address ranges and the reserved
.invalid top-level domain. No live host, malware, or real user data exists.
Do not send packets to any address in the capture.

FILES
- dns_trace.pcap: synthetic Ethernet / IPv4 / UDP / DNS traffic
- scope_notes.txt: address and protocol scope

TOOLS
Wireshark or tshark is recommended. Python 3 can be used for reconstruction.
No external network access is needed.
